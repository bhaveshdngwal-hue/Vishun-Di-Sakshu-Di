I don't really know how to write big emotional paragraphs 😭🤌, but there are some things that genuinely deserve to be said.

You both are not just my cousins. Somewhere between all those childhood memories, random fights, stupid jokes, lockdown madness, teasing each other and spending countless moments together... you became my sisters by heart. 🫶🥹

We have literally grown up together. From those little-kid days to seeing Vishuu Di in CS and Sakshu Di in 12th, it's honestly crazy how quickly time has passed. 😭🤌

Sometimes I really wish we could go back to those childhood days for just one day... sit together, laugh at the dumbest things, create random memories and enjoy everything without worrying about growing up. 🥹🩷

And honestly, lockdown was probably one of the weirdest but best chapters for us. 😂 We somehow managed to turn that boring time into memories that I still remember and miss.

You guys have always been so supportive and understanding. There are things I feel like I can share with you without thinking twice, and that's something I genuinely value. 🫶

And yes... about that block wali baat... I'm sorry 😭😷 Galti sabse hoti hai yaar, hehe. I'm your pyaari si behen after all. 🤭🥰

I may not always know how to express what I feel, but whenever I spend time with you guys, I realize how lucky I am to have these memories. Words honestly aren't enough to explain how special this bond is. 😭🤌🩷

I just hope that as we grow older, we don't become strangers because of studies, careers, responsibilities or life.

  • I hope we still call each other randomly.
  • I hope we still make fun of each other.
  • I hope we still have those stupid conversations that make absolutely no sense. 😂
  • I hope we still take hundreds of pictures together.
  • I hope someday we actually travel together, explore new places, create crazy memories and laugh about all the things we did when we were younger. ✈️🌍🫶

And most importantly... I hope we always make Nani proud. 🥹🤞🩷

No matter where life takes us, I hope this little bond stays the same. Because childhood may be over... but our memories don't have to be. 🫶

Years from now, when we're all busy with our own lives, I hope we can look back at these photos and say:
"Brooo... remember when we were like this? 😭😂"
And then laugh all over again.

So here's my tiny promise:
No matter how much we grow, I'll always be that annoying little sibling who comes back to disturb you both. 🤭😂

Thank you for being part of my childhood.

Thank you for all the laughs.

Thank you for all the support.

Thank you for understanding me.

And thank you for simply being YOU. 🩷
